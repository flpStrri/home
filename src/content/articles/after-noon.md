## Noon

By noon the table has gathered its usual weather: steam on the windows, crumbs in the grain, voices crossing the room and returning changed. The chairs know where everyone sits. Even the empty one seems to be keeping a place.

Outside, the day is making very little progress. A cloud passes over the garden. A bird considers the fence. Inside, somebody reaches for the salt before asking, and somebody else tells the same story from a different beginning.

## After

The plates go quiet one by one. There is a last piece nobody claims, then somebody claims it. Coffee cools beside a newspaper. The table remains after everyone has gone, holding the warmth of the room in its dark wooden surface.

Later, we will remember the meal as a whole. The truth was made of smaller things: the burnt edge, the bright peel of a lemon, the pause before the room began to laugh.
