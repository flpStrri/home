## The first mark

There is always a first mark: a door opened, a light left on, a name written in the margin of an otherwise empty page. This is ours.

The house is quieter than it was an hour ago. Someone has taken the cups away. Somewhere, a tap is still deciding whether to drip. On the table, a folded list waits for tomorrow and a crumb has become, by nightfall, a small piece of history.

## An ordinary archive

We will keep the things that do not announce themselves. A walk taken because the weather changed. A sentence overheard while washing up. The particular blue of the evening window.

Nothing here needs to be important. It only needs to have happened, and to have stayed with us long enough to be written down.
